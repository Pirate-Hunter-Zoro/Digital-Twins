            * Logistic Regression, Decision Trees, etc. for recognized baseline

    - What I am doing
        - Vectorizing all the patients via an embedder
        - We have random sample of 500 TRD positive and 500 TRD negative patients
        - For each anchor patient:
            - Vary neighborhood Scheme
                * Nearest neighbors (cosine similarity)
                * Random neighbors
                * Farthest neighbors (cosine similarity)
                * Subsample neighbors (random bigger sample of patients, and out of them pick the nearest)
            - Vary the weighting Strategy
                * Uniform
                * Cosine similarity weighting -> [-1, 1] -> (0.8, 1.0)
                * LLM similarity weighting
                * Combined (Harmonic Mean of cosine and LLM similarity)
            - To get a trd_risk_score for a patient
                * Each neighbor has a TRD 0/1 flag -> f (vector)
                * Each neighbor has a weight**alpha -> w (vector)
                * Anchor patient's risk score is (w dotted with f) / (sum of w)
        - For all of our anchor patients, we have this score
