- [ ] Vitals missingness investigation + asymmetric imputation refactor.
      All four FEATURE feature_importance PNGs show `num__bmi` (and
      `num__bp_sys` in random_forest) painted RED — i.e. the model
      learned that rising BMI / systolic BP LOWERS predicted TRD risk.
      Literature flatly disagrees: higher BMI and metabolic burden
      trend toward worse depression outcomes, not better. Suspected
      confound: SimpleImputer(strategy="median") at classical_ml.py:72
      slams every missing vital onto the cohort median, conflating
      "no recorded vital" (a data-richness signal) with "patient is at
      the cohort-typical value" (a clinical signal). Work proceeds in
      three steps and MUST run in order:
        * Step 1 — Vitals missingness diagnostic in
          notebooks/cohort_investigation.ipynb. The notebook already
          carries the vital_keys list (SystolicBloodPressure,
          DiastolicBloodPressure, PulseRate, RespirationRate,
          WeightInGrams, HeightInInches, TemperatureInFahrenheit,
          BodyMassIndex) and a per-patient encounter-scan skeleton
          whose inner loop body is currently `pass`. Compute the
          percent of patients missing each vital across the cohort,
          and cross-tab missingness against the TRD label so we know
          whether the missingness pattern itself carries outcome
          signal — that determines whether LR-side imputation is
          salvageable as-is or needs a MissingIndicator augmentation.
        * Step 2 — Asymmetric refactor of `make_classifier` in
          classical_ml.py. Tree-based learners can handle NaN
          natively, so the median imputer should be DROPPED from
          their preprocessing branches:
            - RandomForestClassifier: native NaN support landed in
              sklearn 1.4; confirm ehr_env version before dropping.
            - GradientBoostingClassifier: does NOT handle NaN. Either
              swap to HistGradientBoostingClassifier (NaN-aware) or
              leave the imputer in place — pick after Step 1.
            - XGBClassifier: native NaN, drop the imputer.
            - LogisticRegression: still needs imputation. Strategy
              choice (plain median vs median + MissingIndicator vs
              something else entirely) is contingent on Step 1 — do
              NOT pick until the missingness rates and TRD-stratified
              cross-tab are in hand.
          Easiest implementation path is to parameterize
          `make_classifier` by classifier flavor (imputer-yes vs
          imputer-no) rather than maintaining four hand-tuned
          pipelines — but that's an implementation detail; either
          works.
        * Step 3 — Re-run analysis_only.sbatch end-to-end against the
          refactored pipeline. Re-inspect the four
          feature_importance PNGs and confirm `num__bmi` / `num__bp_sys`
          no longer carry the literature-incoherent red direction.
          If they still do, the confound was somewhere else and the
          investigation continues.
