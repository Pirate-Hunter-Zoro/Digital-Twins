- [ ] VITALS STEP 2 — Advanced vitals imputation arms. Sibling
      sensitivity strategies to the Step 1 median-IMPUTE arm; extends
      the IMPUTE family with multivariate imputers rather than
      adding a new top-level strategy. Median imputation collapses
      every missing vital onto the cohort median; multivariate
      imputers can in principle extract more signal by interpolating
      each missing cell from its row's other features. Candidates:
        * KNN imputation (`sklearn.impute.KNNImputer`) — uses the
          k nearest patient rows in feature space to interpolate
          missing vital values.
        * MICE — Multivariate Imputation by Chained Equations
          (`sklearn.experimental.IterativeImputer` or the `mice` R
          port). Iteratively regresses each missing column on the
          others until convergence.
      Both presume within-row correlation between the missing vital
      and the patient's other features.

      Prerequisites (both required before Step 2 work begins):
        * Step 1 landed and green — the per-classifier legal-arm
          sweep with median IMPUTE must already be merged and
          producing the 9-row vitals_sensitivity.csv. Step 2 plugs
          into the same aggregator and JSON-emit conventions Step 1
          establishes.
        * cohort_investigation.ipynb packaging diagnostic confirms
          vitals are NOT missing in packages (all three at once).
          If the packaged-missingness hypothesis holds, row-wise
