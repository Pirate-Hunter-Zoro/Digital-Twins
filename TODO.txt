- ABLATION STRATEGY PIVOT: NEUTRALIZE -> COHORT PERMUTATION.
    * Mentor pushback: writing "Missing" / "Absent" / "0" into a
      populated field plants *false* missing information. The
      embedder may have learned distinct semantics for genuine
      missingness; injecting that token where there was a real
      value confounds the ablation signal with a missingness
      signal we did not intend to measure.
    * NEW STRATEGY: for each anchor patient, replace the perturbed
      field's value with the same field's value drawn from a
      randomly chosen DONOR patient in the cohort. The embedder
      sees a real-looking value; the field's correlation with the
      anchor's outcome is severed; the field's marginal
      distribution is preserved exactly (sampled from the empirical
      marginal, not a synthetic placeholder).
    * Standard permutation-importance trick (Breiman 2001) lifted
      from the feature-vector level to the embedder-input level.
      Conceptually: scramble that one column across patients, then
      re-embed.
    * Section-level ablation: draw ONE donor per anchor and copy
      EVERY field in the section jointly. Preserves intra-section
      coherence so the embedder reads a clinically plausible block,
      not a Frankenstein mosaic. Single-donor-per-section is the
      default. Multi-donor-per-section (each field permuted
      independently) is strictly stronger but breaks intra-section
      structure and may confound the ablation with coherence loss.
      Deferred.
    * Override-spec format updates (registry dict in the future
      ablation module):
        - field-level:   {"field": "<field_name>",
                          "strategy": "permute"}
        - section-level: {"section": "<section_header>",
                          "strategy": "permute_section"}
      No more {"value": "Missing"} static replacement.
    * deterministic_narrative chokepoint now needs three new
      inputs: (a) the anchor patient ID, (b) access to the cohort
      donor pool (or at minimum the subset of patients holding
      that field non-null), (c) a seeded RNG keyed on
      (anchor_id, ABLATION_ID, SEED) for reproducibility per
      anchor per ablation. Self-donor MUST be excluded so every
      perturbation is genuine.
    * Length and positional encoding stay stable: section headers
      and field labels are preserved; only values move. Original
      design's length/position confounds remain solved.

- "WHICH NAMED CLINICAL CONCEPT MATTERS ON THE EMBEDDING SIDE"
  is answered by the ablation study, NOT by feature_importance.py.
  feature_importance.py answers "what does the trained classifier
  weigh on its inputs"; the embedding classifier sees 4096
  entangled latents, not named clinical columns, so per-concept
  attribution is mathematically unavailable from the classifier
  alone. The ablation study sidesteps this by perturbing the
  *embedder's input* (the narrative) and reading the downstream
  AUC delta — a pipeline-level rather than classifier-level
  question. Important conceptual distinction; locked in for
  future-Claude / future-self.

- classical_ml.py was wall-clock-starved on 8 CPUs. LR alone ran >1 hour
  before the fix. Diagnosed and addressed on three axes:
    * SBATCH: run_trd_prediction_analysis.sbatch bumped from
      --cpus-per-task=8 -> 48 and --mem=64G -> 192G. N_THREADS on line 16
      auto-inherits SLURM_CPUS_PER_TASK. GridSearchCV(n_jobs=-1) now has
      48 workers instead of 8. c3_accel partition allows up to 96; 48 is
      the conservative choice.
    * LR grid thinned: elasticnet sub-grid's model__l1_ratio collapsed
      from 6 values [0.15, 0.3, 0.45, 0.6, 0.75, 0.9] to 3 values
      [0.25, 0.5, 0.75]. Drops elasticnet from 36 combos to 18. LR total
      goes from 62 combos (310 fits) to 44 combos (220 fits).
    * SVM dropped entirely from the classifier lineup. Reasoning:
        - On embedding source (4096-dim): kernel methods suffer curse of
          dimensionality; rbf Gram matrix degenerates; linear SVM
          essentially duplicates LR with hinge loss.
        - On deterministic source: tree ensembles already capture
          nonlinearity on tabular mixed-dtype features; SVM on
          one-hot-encoded demographics offers no unique signal.
        - Compute disproportionate: SVC(probability=True) is
